![[logo.png]]
I recently participated in [LA CTF](https://lac.tf/) as a member of [defpwn](https://ctftime.org/team/316048), the CTF team for SFU's newly-formed cybersecurity club. This was the club's first event together and most of our team's first time encountering a CTF, hence we were all n00bs. Regardless, I think it was a good experience for everyone involved. 

![[event_details.png]]

The event ran for 42 hours; however, we only met for the last 4 hours. Rather than work remotely and async, we met IRL synchronously to get to know each other and help others out if they needed a hand. 

![[defpwn_score.png]]

Out of nearly 1000 teams, we placed 444th - nothing to brag about (but at least we weren't last!).

![[defpwn_results.png]]

In the end, we solved 9/55 challenges (2 of which were the trivial welcome challenges). Of the 7 remaining challenges, I completed 3:
1. crypto/[[big e]]
2. crypto/[[Extremely Convenient Breaker]]
3. misc/[[extended]]

I had hoped to contribute more than these three, but with the clock ticking, this was all I had in me. In any case, it's a good baseline for future events to see how I improve!
